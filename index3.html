In the Philippines, cyber libel is libel committed through a computer system or similar digital means, punishable under Section 4(c)(4) of Republic Act No. 10175 (the Cybercrime Prevention Act of 2012). For more details on filing or defending a claim, see STLAF Global.
In the Philippines, the primary law governing money laundering is the Anti-Money Laundering Act of 2001 (AMLA), enacted as Republic Act No. 9160.
Under Republic Act No. 10591 (the Comprehensive Firearms and Ammunition Regulation Act of the Philippines), the unlawful acquisition or possession of firearms and ammunition carries strict criminal penalties depending on the type and number of weapons involved
Presidential Decree No. 1602 (P.D. 1602), commonly referred to as the Anti-Illegal Gambling Law of the Philippines, was issued by President Ferdinand E. Marcos on June 11, 1978, to prescribe stiffer penalties for illegal gambling and betting
